Which portal should lifecycle tickets go to?
You own 3 portals in Service Desk. Pick the one your IT and facilities teams already work in.

Why this is needed
Workforce Lifecycle does not have its own queue.

When someone joins, moves, or leaves, this space creates the tickets that make it happen: order a laptop, cut a badge, grant an application, revoke access on the last day. Those tickets need a portal to live in, with its own queues, assignees, and SLAs.

Until a portal is connected, nothing in this space can create work, which is why the dashboard reads zero.
